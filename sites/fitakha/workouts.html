Technique, posture & movement

Every exercise page lists setup, execution cues and common mistakes. If you're still unsure, a coach can review your technique directly.

Progression

We build consistency first, then gradually increase repetitions, resistance or session length as your performance and recovery allow — for example, bodyweight squat → goblet squat → barbell squat.

High-complexity movements are held back for never-exercised users unless a coach selects them — this keeps early sessions safe and achievable.

Recovery & using Fitakha

Fitakha reduces session intensity and volume and favors technique, mobility or recovery work when readiness is low — the same logic a careful coach would apply.
