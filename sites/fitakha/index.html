How it works

Tell us about yourself → explore suitable options → start moving

1

Tell us about yourself

Share your goal, experience, available equipment, time and any relevant health context. No account required to start.

2

Explore suitable options

Fitakha's rules engine filters for safety and eligibility first, then matches you to a program, exercises and FAQs that fit.

3

Start moving

Follow along with Be a Fitakha videos, track your consistency, and connect with a coach or nutritionist whenever you want more support.
