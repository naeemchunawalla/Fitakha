Vimlesh Mittal

Founder & CEO, Fitakha

Vimlesh Mittal brings 28 years of experience in architecting and building IT solutions for clients, alongside a personal commitment to working out that spans three decades. His own fitness journey has inspired him to encourage and help others become fitter. As a NASM Certified Personal Trainer, he puts his education into practice by helping people develop better exercise habits and make more informed choices about their fitness.

That vision began with a question familiar to many fitness enthusiasts: “What workout is right for my goals?” Through years of training, mistakes, and injuries, Vimlesh learned the importance of exercise technique, recovery, sleep, balanced nutrition, and knowing when to rest. These experiences inspired him to help others make more informed choices and avoid preventable setbacks.

Through Fitakha, Vimlesh is drawing on his 28 years of designing and delivering technology solutions to build an ecosystem that connects practical fitness knowledge with expert guidance. He brings together his IT expertise, NASM education, and lessons from his own workout journey to help people access guidance suited to their goals, abilities, and everyday lives—wherever they are and whatever equipment they have.

He believes access to fitness should not depend on a gym membership or expensive equipment. Whether someone is training at home, in a gym, or on the move, his vision is to make reliable guidance and opportunities to improve well-being available to everyone, from anywhere.

Jennifer Giron

Fitness Program Architect, Fitakha

Jennifer Giron brings a strong foundation in kinesiology, exercise science, and client-centered wellness to her role as Fitness Program Architect at Fitakha. She will help shape personalized, science-informed fitness programs that support diverse abilities, goals, and stages of life.

Jennifer holds a Bachelor of Education in Kinesiology and Exercise Science from the University of Kentucky, along with certifications as an ACSM Exercise Physiologist and NCSF Personal Trainer, and in BLS and CPR/AED. Her experience spans cardiac rehabilitation, fitness assessments, individualized exercise programming, and health education.

Through her work as an Assistant Manager and Fitness Specialist, Jennifer helps clients build strength, confidence, and independence. At Fitakha, she brings this practical experience to designing accessible, progressive programs that help people move safely and improve their overall well-being.

Arti Agrawal

Marathon Coach, Fitakha

Arti Agrawal is an ultramarathon runner, Team India athlete, ACSM Certified Coach, and founder of Cadence Running Community. She brings firsthand endurance racing experience and a passion for helping runners achieve their goals to her role as Marathon Coach at Fitakha.

In 2026, Arti became the first Indian woman to finish the Spartathlon, completing the legendary 246 km race from Athens to Sparta in 35 hours and 18 minutes. She has represented India at the IAU 24-Hour World Championships in France and earned multiple podium finishes in marathons and ultramarathons, including demanding 100-mile races.

A full-time banking professional and mother, Arti understands the dedication required to balance training with everyday responsibilities. Through Cadence Running, she coaches runners worldwide toward their marathon and ultramarathon goals. At Fitakha, she brings this experience to helping runners build endurance, develop confidence, and train with purpose—from their first marathon to new personal milestones.

Charlie Sinnott

Certified Personal Trainer, Fitakha

Charlie Sinnott holds a Bachelor of Science in Fitness and Wellness Leadership from Towson University in Towson, Maryland. Charlie is a Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) through the NCCA-accredited American Council on Exercise (ACE) and holds CPR certification through the American Public Health Association (APHA).

Charlie specializes in traditional strength and conditioning, with a strong focus on muscle building (hypertrophy), weight loss, and injury prevention. His training expertise spans anaerobic resistance training, strength and power training, agility and speed training, high-intensity interval training, and aerobic endurance training.

Charlie has worked with men, women, and children across ages and fitness levels, including clients in corporate, government, law enforcement, and youth sports settings. He enjoys both individual and group training, in person and virtually.

Known for professionalism, optimism, and compassion, Charlie designs personalized fitness programs around each client's goals and wellness needs. He emphasizes proper exercise and lifting techniques to help clients train safely and effectively.

At Fitakha, Charlie's objective is to support, challenge, and educate clients about fitness guidelines, exercise techniques, and the benefits of training in relation to their individual programs and goals.

A little clarity

Good questions. Simple answers.

Can I explore without creating an account?

Yes. Try our guest discovery flow and browse the exercise links freely. Visit Log in / Register to explore the account experience. Full Fitakha accounts, saved programs, and cross-device progress are planned for a future release.

Is Fitakha purely AI?

No. The planned Fitakha Intelligence Engine uses proprietary rules to filter exercise suitability first. AI-assisted personalization then works within those boundaries. This website's discovery tool is a simple guided preview, not the production recommendation engine.

Can this assess or diagnose my posture?

The guest flow includes an optional posture and movement self-check before exercise guidance. It records your own observations and does not diagnose posture or muscle imbalances. A qualified professional can perform a movement assessment and help interpret your individual needs.

Do I need gym equipment or a wearable?

No. The Fitakha vision includes options for home, gym, and travel, with or without equipment. Wearable data is an optional input for future personalization.

How can I contact the team or become a partner?

Still need help? Visit Contact Us for inquiries, site support, or feedback. Expert booking, payments, and account registration are not yet available in this preview.
