About Fitakha

Our story, our mission, our approach, and the team building Fitakha — progress with purpose, for everyone who's ready to move.

Our story

Life changes. Fitness can grow with you.

New responsibilities. New relationships. New rhythms. We believe caring for yourself deserves a place in every chapter of life — not just the chapters with spare time.

Fitakha brings movement, mindset, nutrition and recovery into one connected vision of wellbeing, taught clearly through the Be a Fitakha channel and backed by real coaches and nutrition professionals — with space for your ambitions and your everyday reality.

"A stronger you starts with a little time for you."

01. Movement

Strength, mobility, balance, and activities you enjoy.

02. Mindset

Encouragement, connection, and habits you can return to.

03. Nutrition

Practical food education and a path to qualified nutrition support.

04. Recovery

Room for rest, reflection, and sustainable progress.

Our mission

Progress with purpose

1. Make fitness accessible for everyone

Help people build consistent fitness habits regardless of experience, equipment, or location — with clear demonstrations, proper technique, common mistakes, and adaptable workouts for home, office, gym or travel.

2. Deliver personalized, adaptive guidance

Recommend workouts based on individual goals, abilities, preferences, equipment and relevant health and recovery data — adapting as people progress, across weight management, strength, mobility, general fitness and sports preparation.

3. Connect people with expertise and support

Build an ecosystem where users discover coaches and, over time, nutritionists, dietitians and other qualified wellness professionals — combining education, professional guidance and progress tracking.

Our approach

Personalized starting points, practical education, human support

Behind every recommendation is a simple, deliberate separation of responsibilities — so safety decisions are never left to guesswork.

1

Taxonomy — what things are

A common language for exercises, users, goals, equipment, conditions and movements, so every part of Fitakha understands the same terms consistently.

2

Rules — what's appropriate

Deterministic safety and eligibility rules run first: excluding what doesn't fit your equipment, experience or reported health context, and flagging anything that needs a professional review before continuing.

3

Guidance — what's best for you

From the safe, relevant options that remain, Fitakha personalizes sequencing, load and explanation to your goals, readiness and preferences — and always explains why.

Fitakha's rules are product-policy specifications, reviewed on an ongoing basis, and are not a substitute for individualized clinical or professional advice. Acute red-flag symptoms always route to a stop-and-refer message rather than an automated workout.
