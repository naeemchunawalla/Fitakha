Jennifer Giron | Fitness Program Architect, Fitakha

Jennifer Giron brings a strong foundation in kinesiology, exercise science, and client-centered wellness to her role as Fitness Program Architect at Fitakha. She will help shape personalized, science-informed fitness programs that support diverse abilities, goals, and stages of life.

Jennifer holds a Bachelor of Education in Kinesiology and Exercise Science from the University of Kentucky, along with certifications as an ACSM Exercise Physiologist and NCSF Personal Trainer, and in BLS and CPR/AED. Her experience spans cardiac rehabilitation, fitness assessments, individualized exercise programming, and health education.

Through her work as an Assistant Manager and Fitness Specialist, Jennifer helps clients build strength, confidence, and independence. At Fitakha, she brings this practical experience to designing accessible, progressive programs that help people move safely and improve their overall well-being.
