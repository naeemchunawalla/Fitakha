Arti Agrawal | Marathon Coach, Fitakha

Arti Agrawal is an ultramarathon runner, Team India athlete, ACSM Certified Coach, and founder of Cadence Running Community. She brings firsthand endurance racing experience and a passion for helping runners achieve their goals to her role as Marathon Coach at Fitakha.

In 2026, Arti became the first Indian woman to finish the Spartathlon, completing the legendary 246 km race from Athens to Sparta in 35 hours and 18 minutes. She has represented India at the IAU 24-Hour World Championships in France and earned multiple podium finishes in marathons and ultramarathons, including demanding 100-mile races.

A full-time banking professional and mother, Arti understands the dedication required to balance training with everyday responsibilities. Through Cadence Running, she coaches runners worldwide toward their marathon and ultramarathon goals. At Fitakha, she brings this experience to helping runners build endurance, develop confidence, and train with purpose—from their first marathon to new personal milestones.
