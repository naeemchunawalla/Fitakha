A little clarity

Good questions. Simple answers.

Can I explore without creating an account?

Yes. Try our guest discovery flow and browse the exercise links freely. Visit Log in / Register to explore the account experience. Full Fitakha accounts, saved programs, and cross-device progress are planned for a future release.

Is Fitakha purely AI?

No. The planned Fitakha Intelligence Engine uses proprietary rules to filter exercise suitability first. AI-assisted personalization then works within those boundaries. This website's discovery tool is a simple guided preview, not the production recommendation engine.

Can this assess or diagnose my posture?

The guest flow includes an optional posture and movement self-check before exercise guidance. It records your own observations and does not diagnose posture or muscle imbalances. A qualified professional can perform a movement assessment and help interpret your individual needs.

Do I need gym equipment or a wearable?

No. The Fitakha vision includes options for home, gym, and travel, with or without equipment. Wearable data is an optional input for future personalization.

How can I contact the team or become a partner?

Still need help? Visit Contact Us for enquiries, site support, or feedback. Expert booking, payments, and account registration are not yet available in this preview.
